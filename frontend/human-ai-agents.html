      Questions we get

      Where does the name "Verilume" come from? ▸

      Veri — Latin for truth. Radical accuracy, un-siloed data, honest attribution: cutting through vanity metrics (clicks, impressions) to see actual business impact (LTV, retention, conversion). Lume — light, illumination: truth is useless sitting unread in a dashboard, so illumination is what turns it into where to invest next and what to do about it. Most brands aren't short on data — they're short on clarity, in three places specifically: media optimization (real-time reallocation instead of black-box attribution), customer experience (an honest map of the friction, not another dashboard), and strategy & ops (one shared source of truth instead of marketing and CX each defending their own number).

      Does Kaizen 2.0 apply to my team, or just the AI agents? ▸

      Both — every human seat runs the same discipline as the AI layer: a real skill baseline scored against that seat's own bar, a gap named specifically rather than a vague development plan, and the next campaign shaped by what actually improved. Three pieces of this are built and live in every client portal today: self-rating against the role's own skill bar, a skill-set library by level and function, and a career development checklist that exports as a branded PDF.
