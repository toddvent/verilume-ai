What this policy covers

Verilume is operated by CX Experiences LLC (“CX Experiences,” “Verilume,” “we” or “us”), which is the company responsible for the information described here. This policy explains what information Verilume collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Verilume website, the free assessment, and the Verilume platform.

Information we collect

We collect information in three ways.

  • You give it to us: your name, work email, phone number, company details and role when you register or invite teammates; your assessment answers; and content and files you add to the platform, such as marketing budgets, campaign details, transaction and lead data, brand materials and written content.
  • We collect it as you use the Service: sign-in and security events, device and browser information, pages and features used, and the actions you take, such as approvals and decisions you make on recommendations.
  • From connections you authorize: data from services you link to your account, such as analytics, data warehouse, advertising or call-tracking tools, only as you permit.

How we use information

We use information to provide, secure and support the Service; to create your reports, forecasts, scores and recommendations; to verify your identity and send sign-in codes and service messages; to improve the Service; to communicate with you about your account, trials and packages; and to meet legal obligations. The decisions your team makes in the platform are used to tailor recommendations for your account.

Artificial intelligence

The Service sends the content needed for a task, such as your brand voice materials and campaign details, to AI providers we use to generate, score or summarize content and to answer questions. We share only what a task requires and use providers under terms that govern how they handle that information. AI output is generated automatically and should be reviewed by a person before it is relied on.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it with: service providers that help us operate the Service, such as cloud hosting and database providers, email and text-message delivery providers, payment processors, and AI providers; people in your own organization according to the roles your administrator sets; service connections you authorize; professional advisers; and authorities when the law requires it. If Verilume is involved in a merger, financing or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction, with notice to you where required.

Email and text messages

We send service messages such as sign-in codes, security alerts and reports you request. We send marketing messages only where permitted, and you can opt out from any marketing email using its unsubscribe link. Message and data rates from your carrier may apply to text messages. We will not send text messages beyond what you have agreed to.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, protect against abuse, and understand how the Service is used. You can control cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the Service may not work without the essential ones.

Security

We use access controls by account and role, encryption of data in transit, and other safeguards designed to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Tell us promptly if you suspect a problem with your account.

Connected ad and analytics accounts

You can connect accounts you manage, such as Google Analytics, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ad accounts, Google Ads, and similar services, so Verilume can read their reporting data. When you do:

  • Read-only: we ask only for permission to read reporting data, such as spend, impressions, clicks, sessions, engagement and results by campaign. We do not create, edit, pause or delete anything in your accounts.
  • Used for your account only: the data is used to show you reports and recommendations in your own Verilume account. We do not sell it, share it with other customers, or use it to advertise to anyone.
  • Limited access: access is granted by you, through the connected service’s own sharing controls, to Verilume’s business account or service account. Credentials are stored encrypted and are never shown back.
  • Your control: you can switch off or disconnect at any time by removing Verilume’s access in the connected service (for example, removing the partner or the Viewer access), or by turning off the setting in Train the Brain. We then stop pulling new data.

Where an account contains personal information, we treat it as described in this policy.

Deleting your data

To delete data we hold from a connected account, or to delete your Verilume account data, email privacy@verilume.ai from an address on your account, or ask your account administrator to contact us. Tell us the company and, if you only want specific data removed, which connection (for example, Meta or Google Analytics). We will confirm by email when it is done. We may keep limited records that the law requires, and backups are removed as they expire. If you disconnect a service, previously stored data stays on file until you ask us to delete it.

Retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, meet legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. You can ask us to delete your account data, subject to what we must keep by law.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to withdraw consent. Account administrators can manage much of this inside the platform. To make a request, contact us using the address below. We will respond as the law requires and may need to verify your identity first.

Children

The Service is for business use and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International use

Verilume is operated from the United States. If you use the Service from another country, your information will be processed in the United States and other places where our providers operate, which may have different data-protection laws.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make a material change, we will post the new version with a new date and, where appropriate, notify account administrators.
