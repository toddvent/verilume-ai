Owner Todd Brown Audience Product, engineering leadership, future CTO onboarding Updated 2026-08-27
Two boundary calls made explicit, not silently decided

Copywriting is its own group rather than folded into Marketing/Campaigns — it's the platform's core output, not a campaign feature (see the Copywriting doc's companion vision reference). Team access control (who can log in, admin rights) stays under Data Management > Registration & Account Management; team roster/org profile display is a separate, not-yet-built capability under Company Profile & Team. Revisit either call any time — flagged here so it was a decision, not a default.
