What is "Kaizen 2.0," and how does it apply to my industry? ▸

The word kaizen means "change for the better" — durable improvement from small, measured steps, checked against real results every time, not occasional big swings. A luxury cruise line's gap sits at Purchase; a retail apparel brand's sits at Awareness; a sporting goods franchise's sits at Loyalty. Same discipline, different stage — Verilume diagnoses where your category's promise most often breaks, then runs the loop specifically there, with an AI agent running the always-on version of that work in every seat.
