Framework FAQ

How is this different from a customer feedback survey or NPS tool? ▸

We're not a feedback loop. We're a promise-and-delivery loop. A lot of respected tools in this space exist to capture what a customer says after an experience — a survey, an NPS score, a complaint, a review — and help a team respond to it. That's a real and valuable job. It's also a different job than the one this framework does.

Post-experience feedback tools

Ask the customer, after the fact

Collect what customers say once an experience is already over — surveys, sentiment analysis, complaints, reviews — then route that feedback to the right team to respond to it.

  • Starts with the customer's voice, after the moment has passed
  • Strong at surfacing what already went wrong
  • Doesn't touch the campaign, the creative, or the media plan that set the promise in the first place
Verilume

Measure the promise and the delivery, at the same time

Score what your media is telling people to expect and what your organization actually delivers — the call, the counter, the room, the follow-through — against the same rubric, before you wait for a customer to tell you it broke.

  • Starts with your own marketing and operational data, in real time
  • Strong at catching a gap before it becomes a complaint
  • Lives in the same place you plan campaigns, write creative, and QA the work — not a separate feedback inbox

"A survey tells you the promise already broke. This framework shows you where it's about to."

Do I need to score every stage of the Media Loop, or can I start with one? ▸

Start with one. Most teams begin at the stage they suspect is weakest — often Purchase, since it's the waist of the loop where Media's promise and CX's delivery physically meet. The free assessment gives you an instant quadrant placement; the full framework lets you expand stage by stage from there.

What do I do once I know my quadrant? ▸

The quadrant tells you which side of the gap to close first — media reach or delivery consistency. Human & AI Agents work the Scoring Grid's specific weak cells from there, with a person reviewing and approving every change before it ships.
