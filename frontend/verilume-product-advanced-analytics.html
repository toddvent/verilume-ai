Advanced Analytics

The analytics that run behind the dashboards: a forecast engine that learns how this account's own funnel behaves — impressions to website visits to leads to bookings to revenue — and projects results from spend or impressions. It has no screen of its own. It is part of Train the Brain, and its answers appear in Ask Verilume (typed and voice) and, as the dashboards evolve, in the reporting dashboards.

Owner Todd Brown System backend/forecast-engine.js and backend/server.js Built 2026-09-30 Status Built & tested on synthetic data — not yet run on production data

Behind the scenes

No new screen

Reached through Ask Verilume and the forecast endpoints; each calibration is logged to the AI Brain ledger.

Questions it answers: what ROAS to expect from the 2H-26 plan as loaded; how many leads and bookings a spend or impressions change buys; what one more million impressions is worth; when revenue lands.

The funnel chain§ 01

01

Impressions → website visits

Visits = a monthly baseline (organic and direct) plus a rate per impression. Spend is converted to impressions at the recent blended CPM.

02

Visits → leads

Total leads (forms and calls) divided by visits, month by month.

03

Leads → bookings

Booking rate by lead type on mature lead cohorts only, applied to the mix of lead types seen in the last six months.

04

Bookings → revenue → ROAS

Bookings at the account's average order value; revenue lands after the average days-to-convert. ROAS = forecast revenue ÷ working media. Also cost per visit, lead and booking.

Ranges & confidence§ 03

⚠ The range is a planning band

Low and high put every stage at its own 25th / 75th percentile together. It is a planning band, not a statistical confidence interval, and every forecast says so.

Confidence is high when all four stages are learned from the account's history, medium with three, low with one or two, and “benchmark only” when none are. Data caveats (for example, too few mature lead months) are returned with every forecast, and Ask Verilume is instructed to say the numbers are an estimate, not a guarantee.

What was verified§ 05

Synthetic account, known answers

Built with visits = 20,000 + 0.006 × impressions, 2.5% visit-to-lead, 25% mixed lead-to-booking and a $4,000 order value, the engine recovered every rate, excluded the two lead months still inside the window, fell back to labeled benchmarks for a data-poor account, and the endpoints, forecast-intent prompt, 401 without a session, and once-a-day ledger write all behaved.

⚠ Not yet run on production data

The first real check is Ask Verilume on the live account after deploy.

Not built yet§ 06 — for product awareness

⚠ Inputs not yet feeding the forecast

Customer counts by age and geo, Wealth and generation focus, DM conversion rates (ID resolution vs standard), competitors and battle briefs, and brand tone. These are for the consultant-style media impact commentary and for splitting the forecast by audience.

⚠ Comparisons and cuts

Budgets (2025 / 2026) versus forecast, forecast versus last year's actuals, seasonality, channel-specific rates, product-group and creative-focus forecasts, and creative-asset influence at the bottom of the analytics story.

⚠ Definitions to confirm

“Impressions” in the history is the all-channel media definition (print and direct mail included); revenue is lead-sourced bookings at the account's average order value; new-customer CAC is not defined yet, so cost per booking is reported instead.
