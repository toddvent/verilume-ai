Team Console

Current as of release r114, 2026-11-25

The Team Console is where Verilume staff run the platform: find clients, change levels, open a client's workspace, review AI output and manage the team. It replaces the separate Ops Console. Product groups: Registration & Account Management, and Company Profile & Team.

What it does today

AreaState

Clients tab (levels, trial settings)Built.

Deploy and verification

  • Backend first: server.js and ops-console-app.html. Then frontend: platform.html. /api/health shows the build stamp.
  • Verified on a scratch server and in a headless browser: workspace read, connection save, invoices, agreements, team toggle, level change with IO and Terms, read-only blocked, bad token rejected.
  • Not tested: live Postgres, Railway, Twilio, the embedded panels inside a live team session.

Known limits

  • Postgres folds some column names to lowercase; the new routes read them through a helper. Check the first live open of an account.
  • A team sign-in unlocks every staff route, including routes that previously needed the secret token. Only read-only is restricted.
  • No create-account or archive control in workspaces.
