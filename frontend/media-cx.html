Questions we get

Where do the channel benchmarks come from? ▸

Each channel entry lists the metrics that matter and a benchmark range with the named study behind it. Where no defensible cross-channel source exists, we say so plainly instead of filling in a guess or a single ad platform's self-reported number.

How does budget development by timeframe work? ▸

Scoped, not yet built. The design is one annual media plan broken into quarterly and monthly tranches, unevenly if the business needs it, with dollars allocated first across the five stages of the customer journey and then by channel, always with a human override.

What does the testing recommendation say by budget tier? ▸

Under $5K: no formal test, change one variable and compare wave over wave. $5K to $25K: one clean A/B test on the highest-leverage variable. $25K and up: A/B or multivariate with a holdout group, and geo holdouts above $100K.

What is built today for Media Mix Modeling? ▸

The data layer is live: historical spend, reach and impressions across 18 categories, a partner-ready mapping, CSV export and a readiness readout. The statistical modeling still needs a chosen vendor, which is a business decision, not a build decision.

Is neuroscience-informed measurement part of Verilume? ▸

Not yet. It is a direction we are tracking as a complement to MMM, early roadmap and not scoped. No vendor, method or timeline is set.
