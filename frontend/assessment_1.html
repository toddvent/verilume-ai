    Free assessment — frequently asked questions

    How long does the free Verilume assessment take? ▸

    About 10 minutes. It's five short steps — company basics, a site scan, your audience profile, a quick self-rating against best practice for the funnel stages that matter to you, and contact info — before your quadrant placement and full report are built.

    Do I need to give my email before I see any results? ▸

    No. You can start the assessment with no contact info at all. Registration is a single step partway through the flow, well before your quadrant position and report are generated.

    What is the Verilume Wealth Index? ▸

    A score for how affluent your selected target audience is, relative to the average U.S. household (100 = average). It combines your target household income with a generation-based wealth multiplier sourced from the Federal Reserve's Survey of Consumer Finances, since net worth is heavily age-skewed and a flat income figure alone understates or overstates an older or younger audience's real buying power.

    What do I get at the end of the free assessment? ▸

    A quadrant placement (where your Media effectiveness and Delivery intersect), your estimated market opportunity, a role-by-role map of where a Verilume AI agent would pick up the specific gaps you rated weak, and a downloadable PDF — all before any package or contract. Want to see one filled out first? View a sample report →

    Is my company's data kept separate from other companies using Verilume? ▸

    Yes. Every account gets its own isolated workbook, provisioned fresh — no account's data, scorecard, or usage history is ever visible to, or used to inform, another account's results.
