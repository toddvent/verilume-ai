Questions we get

What is Verilume? ▸

A full marketing organization, not just a report: real org-chart roles across every function group, each with an AI agent beside it, all calibrated against one instrument, the Media Loop. Humans decide. AI recommends.

Does AI replace my marketing team? ▸

No. Every seat, whether an employee, a fractional hire, or a role you have not filled yet, gets a standing AI agent for the always-on, first-draft work. Your team keeps the judgment calls, the relationships, and the final word.

Who makes the final call, the person or the AI? ▸

A person, every time. The AI recommends and drafts, and shows why. Nothing is published, sent or spent until someone with the authority approves it, and you choose how much of each seat the AI runs.

Is the research on this page a result Verilume achieved? ▸

No. The four findings are published industry benchmarks from Bain, BCG, McKinsey and the Pedowitz Group, and the speed estimate is our own working figure that has not been tested on a live account. Each card says which is which.
