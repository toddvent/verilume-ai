Questions we get

What is Verilume? ▸

A full marketing organization, not just a report: real org-chart roles across every function group, each with AI built into every seat, all calibrated against one instrument: the Media Loop (Awareness, Consideration, Purchase, Loyalty, Advocacy) scored against the customer experience layers. It shows exactly where a brand's marketing promise and its actual customer experience stop matching, and gives every function a place to act on it.

Is the assessment really free? ▸

Yes. Your quadrant placement and one headline insight are shown instantly, with no email gate. The full stage-by-stage scorecard unlocks after you enter your email.

How is this different from typical marketing analytics tools? ▸

Most marketing analytics tools measure the signal alone — reach, clicks, channel performance — in isolation from what happens after. Verilume scores the full loop, including the customer experience layers marketing tools don't usually touch, like the call center and in-person delivery, so it shows whether that promise actually held up — not just how the click performed.

Can an agency license Verilume for its own clients? ▸

Yes. Agencies can license the same instrument — the Media Loop, the CX Quadrant, the scoring rubric — to run under their own name for their own clients.

How does Verilume keep client data isolated? ▸

Every account gets a clean, isolated workbook, provisioned fresh with no reuse of another client's structure. No account's data, scorecards, or usage history is ever visible to, or used to inform, another account's results.

Does AI replace my marketing team? ▸

No. Every seat across every function group — an employee, a fractional hire, or a role you haven't filled yet — gets a standing AI assistant for the always-on, first-draft work. Your team keeps the judgment calls, the relationships, and the final word. See Human + AI, built into every seat above for the engagement models you can choose from, function by function.
