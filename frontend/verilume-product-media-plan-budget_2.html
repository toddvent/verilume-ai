Media Plan & Budget

One page in the portal (Annual Media Plan, step mediaplan) where a client sets up each year's marketing budget and turns it into a channel-by-channel, month-by-month plan. Two ways in share one front door: upload the budget file the client already has (auto-recognized, category-mapped, confirmed), or build it by hand from a total — or gross revenue × a target % — through stage weighting, a recommended channel mix, and a monthly curve. A confirmed budget then drives Active Channels, the Plan Pacing / Budget Tracking / Channel Mix reports, and Campaign Creation's channel picker. This document is the current-state reference, verified against the live code on 2026-09-12.

Headline findingsthis cycle

Phase 1 release scope (decided 2026-09-05). Given the lack of omni-channel MMM data per industry covering print, digital and TV, Phase 1 stops at Loop % allocations based on best practices plus a mapping of sub-channels and audience-type targets — the product does not forecast spend at the sub-channel level. Clients upload or manually enter their own sub-channel dollars into a template; the product shows how those dollars sit against the best-practice Loop split. The research recommendation surfaces (channel-by-stage weights applied to dollars, Re-fit, the Kaizen "adjust toward research" panel, Start over) remain in the code behind MP_PHASE1_TEMPLATE_MODE, switched off, to return as MMM data and activated-budget feedback accumulate.

What the client sees in Phase 1: Step 1 working budget → Step 2 best-practice Loop % (editable, industry-specific where a split exists) → Step 3 the sub-channel template by stage (best-practice stage and audience target per sub-channel, client-entered $, running assigned-vs-target per stage, "Your dollars by Loop stage" vs. best practice) → Step 4 monthly curve → Save. An upload fills the template: each category's dollars land on its sub-channel(s) at their best-practice stage — a mapping, not a forecast.

Upload path§ 02

Accepts .csv or .xlsx in whatever layout the client already has — a flat table or a multi-block export that repeats a header per section. The year and scope come from Plan Setup; a second confirmed budget for the same year+scope is refused (HTTP 409).

01

Upload & analyze

The file is stored (uploaded_files), the grid is scanned for month-name header rows (one = flat table; several = multi-block), and category / Working–Non-Working / month / total columns are guessed. Rows whose total equals the sum of their block are flagged as subtotals so they aren't double-counted.

POST /api/accounts/:id/marketing-budget-uploads
02

Import with the best-guess mapping

Runs automatically (mbuConfirmImport()) — the old per-column "confirm each column" panel was removed in Round 4; Step 2 below is the review screen. Line items are written per raw category × month, with the Working/Non-Working status token normalized (WORKING MEDIA, NON-WORKING MEDIA, etc.).

POST /api/accounts/:id/marketing-budget-uploads/:uploadId/confirm
03

Mapping evaluation

Laid out like a financial statement in three columns — A the category (bold), B the amount, C the mapping/label, with one "Please update as needed" header per section. Working media first: each raw category with Verilume's suggested category as an always-editable grouped dropdown in column C (Total Channel → subcategory, §06; an "International" line can map to International — total only). This is where the client assigns sub-channel allocations: a category mapped to a group's "Total/Unspecified" bucket offers "Split into … sub-channels →", which adds that group's sub-channels as editable $ rows; whatever is assigned to them comes out of the bucket (the group header shows the remainder), so sub-channels feed the total and nothing is counted twice. Non-working spend follows underneath in the same columns, no mapping required, with column C an editable name (saved as a display label) and "+ Add category" for lines the file didn't have (saved as line items via POST …/nonworking-lines). Every total sits under column B; a read-only Total marketing spend (working + non-working) closes the screen.

Manual path — the Media Plan wizard§ 03

Four steps inside #mpPlanSection. Step 2 appears once a Working Marketing Budget exists; Steps 3–4 once a channel mix has been built (mpRenderPlanSteps()). Domestic only.

The upload feeds the manual process (R8)

Per direct instruction, the two paths are one process: the upload is a head start on Steps 1–4, not a separate end state. Confirming an upload (or "Build the media plan from this budget" on the Budget detail) runs mpSeedPlanFromUpload(): Step 1 gets the working total and non-working lines; Step 2 gets the Marketing Loop split — the five stage percentages and dollar totals — derived from the uploaded channel mix (or the client's assigned stage dollars), saved as the account's split so the table, the pie and the channel build agree; Step 3 gets channel rows built from the uploaded categories (the Active-Channels crosswalk plus a plan-seeding crosswalk for the additive subcategories, MBD_SEED_CROSSWALK; categories with no media channel — e.g. International total-only — are named on screen and left off the rows); Step 4 gets the monthly curve from the uploaded months. The page opens on Step 2 so the Loop percentages and totals are the first thing seen, and the client reviews, edits and saves exactly as in the manual path. Once the plan exists, the Budget detail shows the refined plan with the upload named as its source; "Re-seed the plan from this budget" starts over from the upload.

1

Budget

Working Marketing Budget — Type an amount, or Calculate from revenue: gross revenue × a target % of working marketing spend (dropdown, 3%–25% in whole steps, default 7%), with an explicit apply. Beside it, read like a financial statement: Non-working Budget (read-only, the sum of the Non-Working Media lines, with a link to add/edit them), Sales Budget (optional), and Total Sales and Marketing (read-only, working + non-working + sales). The chosen % is remembered on the plan (marketingBudgetPct) but is not a live formula. The revenue field reads the same for every industry — the former Travel/Cruise "Purchase Year" copy and recognition schedule were removed as hard-coded client content (R8).

2

Stage Weighting — one full-width card, the Marketing Loop pie inside it

One row per Media Loop stage (Awareness, Consideration, Purchase, Loyalty, Advocacy) showing the default split for reference plus an editable Share % and an editable Budget $ (share × Step 1). Editing either rebalances the other four proportionally so the split always totals 100%, and saves immediately as the account's override (accounts.stageBudgetWeightsOverrideJson). The pie, this table and the channel allocation all read the same numbers (getIndustryStageWeights(); a saved override is used exactly, with no industry nudge). A suggested split from the Business & Revenue Profile survey can be applied in one click when available. The card states what the weights are based on (mpWeightingBasisText()): the account's saved split or the industry/default stage split, industry-specific channel weights where a table exists (Automotive Sales & Service, Financial Services / Banking today) or the cross-industry table otherwise, and — new in R8 — an adjustment for the audience's generation and wealth tier from Company Profile (GENERATION_CHANNEL_AFFINITY / WEALTH_CHANNEL_AFFINITY, directional media-consumption multipliers averaged across the selected segments and renormalized within each stage; editorial heuristics, disclosed as such). At the bottom of this card: "Continue → build channel mix" on a fresh plan; once channel rows exist, "Re-fit channels to this split →" (mpRefitStagesKeepChannels() — keeps every channel's total exactly as it is, uploaded or hand-edited, and re-spreads each channel across the five stages toward the split by iterative proportional fitting; dollars the plan can't place on a channel, e.g. an International total-only line, are never inflated onto other channels, and the achieved split is stated); "Start over from research" (regenerate everything) is demoted to a confirmed text link — the only destructive path on the page (R9).

GET / POST /api/accounts/:id/stage-weights
3

Channel mix — review & adjust

Phase 1 (template mode, live). Each Loop stage is a card with its job in one line, a table of sub-channels (Sub-channel · Audience target · Annual $ · Stage · Remove), an "add a sub-channel" selector (any of the 22 channels; ones whose best-practice stage is elsewhere are labelled "usually …"), and a footer with assigned $ vs. the Loop target from Step 2. MP_SUBCHANNEL_PRIMARY_STAGE holds the best-practice primary stage and audience-type target per sub-channel (e.g. Direct Mail — Prospects → Awareness, new-to-brand prospect lists; Direct Mail — Inquiries → Purchase, hand-raisers; Direct Mail — Past Guests → Loyalty, past customers; Magazines → Awareness, affluent readers; Brand Search → Purchase; Field / ABM → Purchase, travel-agent/trade partners; PR and Partner Media → Advocacy). Step 2's "Continue → set up the sub-channel template" lays out one $0 row per Active channel at its best-practice stage (mpTplBuildFromActiveChannels); the client types dollars, moves rows between stages (same sub-channel in two stages merges on move) and removes rows. A closing card, "Your dollars by Loop stage," shows best-practice % and target $ against assigned $ and share per stage, plus the unassigned remainder of the working budget (an International total-only line shows here as unassigned, by design). Saving uses the same plan/allocation model as before. Everything below describes the research-recommendation mode, kept in code and switched off in Phase 1. A card like the other steps (R8), with the Full Year / quarter / month View budget selector at the top of the funnel-stage sections it filters. applyRecommendedMix() splits each stage's dollars across its channels by effectiveStageChannelWeights() (industry-specific where one exists, audience-adjusted), blended toward the account's own prior-year channel shares when history exists, limited to Active Channels (§08), and dropping channels that would fall under their minimum-viable spend (listed as "Not recommended this pass"). Every row's Annual $ and Est. CPM are editable; an inline "Add a channel" card adds any stage+channel. The per-channel "playbook" block (audience / creative / KPI notes) is no longer rendered here — it read as campaign-level content (R8).

Adjust toward research — one step at a time (R9, Kaizen). The client's own mix — uploaded or hand-edited — is the anchor; the plan is never rebuilt from scratch behind the client's back. A panel at the top of this step (mpRenderKaizenPanel) lists every channel with its audience target (the account's selected Segments the channel can reach, from SEGMENT_CHANNEL_ELIGIBILITY, else the row's Direct Mail list type), Today (what the client spends), Research suggests (computeRecommendedMix() for the same dollars on channel rows), and After this step. A step size of 10 / 25 / 50 % moves every channel that fraction of the way toward the research figure; mpApplyKaizenStep() scales each existing channel's rows so its stage spread and audience target are untouched, brings a channel the client doesn't run yet in on the stages the research puts it on — but only when research gives it at least 5% of the mix (smaller suggestions are listed as "also suggested, add by hand"), so one step never sprinkles a dozen small rows into the plan — and renormalises so the total on channel rows never changes. The panel states the dollars each step moves; nothing is saved until Save plan, and the step can be repeated or stopped at any point. Seeding from an upload (mpSeedPlanFromUpload) applies the same audience logic: when a category maps to several sub-channels, dollars lean toward the sub-channels that reach the selected Segments (channels no selected Segment can reach keep a dampened 35% weight rather than being dropped). Each channel row in the stage sections now shows its audience target inline.

Sub-channel amounts carry forward (R9). Dollars the client assigns on the review screen with "Split into sub-channels" (e.g. Direct Mail — Customers / Customers Pending / Inquiries / Prospects / Identity Resolution) are saved as their own lines by POST …/category-overrides, come back in byVerilumeCategory, and land on the matching plan channel when the plan is seeded (MBD_SEED_CROSSWALK now covers every Direct Mail sub-channel: Customers and Customers Pending → Past Guests list, Identity Resolution → Prospects list; the group's remaining Total/Unspecified dollars split across the group's channels by research). The Budget detail's Loop stages use the same crosswalk, so the review screen, the detail card and the plan agree. Re-confirming the review screen re-seeds the plan when it is still an unsaved draft or was itself seeded from the upload; a saved, hand-edited plan is kept and the "Re-seed the plan from this budget" action on the Budget detail card is offered instead.

Sub-channels activate channels (R9b). The backend crosswalk (VERILUME_TO_MMM_CHANNEL_MAP) now covers every group and sub-channel category — Digital Total/Addressable/Partnerships, every Direct Mail list, TV Total/Addressable, Consumer Print Total/Magazines, Newspapers — so a budget carrying Magazines, Newspapers, Programmatic Display or a Direct Mail list reaches Active Channels, the Media Plan gate and the research recommendation. The year's confirmed budget is recomputed live by GET …/active-channels?year=, and on every Media Plan load any channel that budget carries is switched on in Active Channels (union only — a channel the client switched on by hand is never switched off), so an already-confirmed budget picks up the fix without re-confirming.

Luxury cruise channel table (R9b). LUXURY_CRUISE_STAGE_CHANNEL_WEIGHTS is the industry table for Cruise Lines — Luxury: Direct Mail (prospects, inquiries, past guests), Magazines, PR, travel-agent trade (Field / ABM), Partner Media and owned email lead; search, social and programmatic play supporting roles. With no industry table the account fell to the cross-industry table, whose Consideration/Purchase rows are search/social/programmatic-led and carry no Magazines and little Direct Mail, which no audience multiplier could correct — the "digital-only" rebuild. Editorial starting table, disclosed as such on screen through mpWeightingBasisText(); the generation/wealth multipliers still apply on top. Verified with the real 2025 budget ($6.82M on channel rows): the from-scratch recommendation is now Direct Mail $3.05M, Consumer Print $1.27M, Digital $1.56M, Trade $0.27M, Email $0.68M — close to the client's own mix, and a 25% Kaizen step moves only $296k.

Monthly distribution, then Save plan

One curve for every channel: Flat (even), Revenue curve (the account's AOV seasonality, editable per month) or Custom %. Stored as monthlyMode flat | curve | manual plus the month table. Save plan writes the plan, its allocations and periods; saving is what flips Plan Setup to Active.

Leverage previous year§ 04

One picker with two kinds of source, so there is a single carry-forward affordance rather than two:

  • A prior confirmed upload (any year/scope) — copied server-side into a new upload for the target year+scope as a draft (pending_category_mapping), never auto-confirmed: last year's numbers are very likely stale, and a silent copy shouldn't rewrite Active Channels without a human looking. It opens on the same mapping-review screen as a real upload. POST …/marketing-budget-uploads/carry-forward
  • A prior saved Media Plan (Domestic) — copied client-side by mpCarryForwardFromYear() into an unsaved draft (fresh allocation ids, months recomputed, Non-Working vendors deliberately not copied) that opens in the Manual steps for review and Save.

International scope & total-only§ 05

International is a different office with the same media-mix opportunity, so budgets are keyed on (accountId, year, scope) — a Domestic and an International budget for the same year coexist, each with its own upload/mapping flow and its own Active/Setup status. Pre-existing rows read as Domestic via COALESCE(scope,'domestic').

For an organization that doesn't want a full International plan, Plan Setup offers "Just track a total budget (skip channel detail)": one number, stored as an auto-confirmed upload flagged totalOnly with a single line item mapped to Other/Uncategorized. Its Active state shows the figure with an "Edit total" action instead of a channel-level detail screen. The channel-level Media Plan builder (Manual path) is Domestic-only for now.

⚠ International does not drive Active Channels

recomputeAccountActiveChannels() runs for Domestic confirms only. Because it replaces the account's channel set wholesale, letting both scopes drive it would mean whichever confirmed last silently wins. How International spend should combine with Domestic for Campaign Creation's channel picker is an open product decision (§11).

Active Channels gate§ 08

The Active Channels tiles (state.activeChannels, persisted on the account profile) are the single gate on the recommended channel mix: only selected channels receive allocation. Confirming a Domestic upload rewrites the account's set to the channels its mapped, nonzero categories crosswalk to (account_active_channels, plus the always-active owned channels) and returns it, so the tiles update immediately and categories not mapped are unselected automatically. A channel can still be added to a plan by hand regardless of the gate. GET /api/accounts/:id/active-channels?year= reports the year's upload-derived set for Campaign Creation.
