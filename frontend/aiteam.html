Questions we get

What is Kaizen 2.0? ▸

The same improvement discipline applied to both the AI and the people. Every correction is kept, so the next draft starts from what was learned, and the AI Brain returns outside knowledge to the team so the team improves too.

Does Kaizen 2.0 apply to my team, or just the AI agents? ▸

Both. Every human seat runs the same discipline as the AI layer: a real skill baseline scored against that seat's own bar, a gap named specifically rather than a vague development plan, and the next campaign shaped by what actually improved.

Who approves what the AI Brain learns? ▸

A person. Campaign lessons apply at once for the campaign. Account-wide and always-on lessons wait for an account admin. Nothing from outside the team is added until someone approves it.

How does the blind contest work? ▸

Your voice inclusions and exclusions, plus the writing style you ask for, go to five external models, shown as LLM Contestant 1 to 5. You read them without knowing which model wrote which, pick a winner, and then see how each draft scored on truth (is it grounded in what is on file) and light (does it show the reader what to do next).

How long does setup take? ▸

About twenty minutes: upload your voice guide, note your vendors, add playbooks, link past campaigns, add budgets, historical analysis and media benchmarks, and map team roles. After that, every correction makes the AI Brain better at your business.
