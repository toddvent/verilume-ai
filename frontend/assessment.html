    What the industry's models tell us

    Marketing mix models don't agree on one universal number for how much any single channel — PR, television, search — contributes to sales; that split shifts too much by category and by brand to cite honestly as one figure. What the research does agree on, across nearly a thousand case studies in the IPA effectiveness databank, is the split between the two jobs media does: roughly 60 percent of budget toward long-term brand-building and 40 percent toward short-term activation outperforms other mixes over time. (Binet & Field, IPA) Keep that balance in mind as you rate the team below — a channel that looks weak on immediate response may still be doing its share of the long-term work.
