Website Category Scan

Crawls a company's own website — homepage plus a small allowlist of product/transaction and press pages (not the whole site) — and extracts seven generalized positioning categories from what's actually on those pages. Nothing here is saved, and no account record is read or written. Built to preview how this would eventually inform competitor/positioning context, before that context has any account data behind it.

Page selection is not a full crawl: the homepage, plus up to 11 pages the model picks from the site's real sitemap URLs (fetching a sitemap index's child sitemaps too) as likely product/transaction or press pages, judging only from the URL path. Every picked page is a real URL that was actually found — nothing is invented. Any product/offer data embedded as schema.org JSON-LD on a fetched page is also parsed and fed into extraction, in addition to title/meta/headings/excerpt text.
