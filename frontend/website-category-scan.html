Website Category Scan

Crawls a company's own website — homepage plus a small allowlist of product/transaction and press pages (not the whole site) — and extracts seven generalized positioning categories from what's actually on those pages. Nothing here is saved, and no account record is read or written. Built to preview how this would eventually inform competitor/positioning context, before that context has any account data behind it.

Page selection is an allowlist, not a full crawl: the homepage, plus any sitemap URL whose path matches a product/transaction pattern (itinerary, ship, destination, cruise, voyage, expedition, fleet, shop, product, collection, wine, spirit, gear, store, catalog) or a press pattern (press, news, media), capped at 12 pages. Everything else — investor relations, careers, legal, generic corporate — is excluded by construction.
