2. What each check does

Name is used the same way

Compares the brand name in titles and descriptions with your confirmed brand names. A close variant that is not confirmed is shown for the team to confirm or fix on the page.

Pages say what they are

For the pages with the most search impressions: a title unique to the page, a description present, and the page's main group or destination named in the title. Uses the site check data.

Pages match the voice and facts

Titles and descriptions are checked for avoided terms from the voice guide, and for numbers, prices or superlatives that are not in your approved facts. The team decides whether the page or the fact is wrong.

What people add to your name

Groups brand searches by the word added to the name, such as reviews, cancel, refund, problems, or a competitor. Reputation words are flagged when a page does not answer them. This is demand, not sentiment.

Approved FAQs match current facts

When a fact behind an approved FAQ is edited or removed, the FAQ is marked "Review" and the answer is shown next to the changed fact. It is never edited automatically.

Not included: sentiment or review content (needs a licensed source, so it belongs in the partner area), competitor pages, and image or logo checks.

Decisions for you

  1. Page scope. Check the top 10 pages by search impressions that are in the site check, or every page in it?
  2. Add-on words. Use a standard list (reviews, cancel, refund, complaints, problems, scam, login) plus competitor names from Competitive Positioning, with the team able to add words?
  3. Who resolves a result. Any team member can mark a result "Fixed" (it is re-checked on the next site check), or only an admin?
  4. Fact drift. Mark an approved FAQ "Review" automatically when its fact changes?
  5. Priorities. Should "Needs attention" results go into Priorities by one click as sketched, or only appear in the card?
