Customer Journey FAQ

What are the "two promises" every campaign makes? ▸

Before a campaign gets QA'd against its objective, it's already making two promises at once: what the media says, and what the experience actually delivers when someone shows up. We score both independently, then show exactly where they stop matching — the baseline every campaign gets checked against before its cross-department alignment score even runs. The waist of the loop is PURCHASE — the one stage where what you promised and what you deliver physically meet, usually in the form of a person. See the full Media Loop →

Why does campaign transparency in one place matter? ▸

One place, not four reports. Function status, brand approvals, and (when available) URL health all live on the same campaign — no reconciling three separate dashboards to know where things stand.

Named, not hidden. Every component of the score is labeled and shown at its own percentage — so "why is this campaign not aligned yet" always has a specific, checkable answer.

One weakest link, named. Rather than eyeballing several bars, the synthesis line tells you exactly which input to fix first — the same "synthesize, don't just display" approach used across the portal's dashboard.
