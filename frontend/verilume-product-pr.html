Press releases and the brief§ 02

Create with a title, a release type (Product Launch or Enhancement, Earnings Announcement, Strategic Partnership, Charity or Person Partnership, Significant Hire or Departure), an audience (Consumer or Business and Trade Network), an optional campaign and an optional call to action with a mode: Use for inspiration (default), Use exact copy, or Both. The brief has four parts, 1. The News, 2. The Experience, 3. The Details, 4. The Next Step, stored in briefJson; the row shows "Brief N of 4" or "Brief complete". The News part is required to draft or run a contest.

AI draft and LinkedIn post§ 03

draftPrCorpCommCopyViaAI reads the Voice Guide, Vision, a long-form example, PR writing samples (category pr), past decisions, Terms to Avoid and the brand profile. Competitor positioning is internal-only and never named. The draft is editable; drafting again replaces the working copy.

Optimize for search§ 04

Sits between Draft and Review and is optional. "Find search improvements" reads the Train the Brain Search keywords module (lists, CSV upload, Avoid always wins, top 500, volume bands). Suggestions are tagged SEO, AEO or GEO. Accepted edits apply with "Apply accepted edits to the draft". Search never blocks approval.

Approve, reject and status§ 05

Approve and Reject copy record a decision with a reason. Status is separate: "Mark Distributed" sets Distributed plus distributedDate; "Revert to Draft" undoes it. Any signed-in team member can do all of this; the Enterprise tag is a label, not a lock.

PR Contest§ 06

Ask dock, Contest, "PR and corporate communications", Choose (askDockContestOpenPr). Each row shows a readiness label. "Run contest" calls POST /api/accounts/:id/<endpoint>/:id/interview: one draft from Anthropic plus one per configured AI vendor, built only from the brief (press), key points (corporate) or outlet and topic (editorial). It counts toward the weekly interview cap (429 when reached). "Use this draft" calls .../interview/:interviewId/select, saves the draft as the working copy and records the pick in contest_rankings. Add my own entry and scoring are not built for PR.

Fixed 2026-10-05: drafts were named

The panel said drafts are numbered, not named, but showed vendor labels such as "Anthropic (baseline)", and the baseline was always first. The list is now labelled Draft N and shuffled once per contest. The server still returns vendor labels and keys in the response; the blinding is client-side only.

Editorial pitches and corporate communications§ 07

A pitch is an outlet plus a topic; Draft with AI returns a subject and two or three paragraphs, under 200 words. Corporate communications take a title, a free-text audience and key points ("Save key points" is the only fact source).

Reputation Monitoring and Specialty Partners§ 08

The weekly sweep runs Monday early morning and is ready by 8:00 AM. Needs Response comes only from Perplexity results. There is no listening vendor feed and no respond-to-mention workflow. Brandwatch, Meltwater and Mention buttons are in-memory UI state.

Search Everywhere hand-offs§ 08a

New columns on press_releases: searchSeedJson and publishedUrl (both in schema-identifiers.json). The list endpoint adds searchSeed and, for Distributed rows, searchStatus (prSearchStatus): on-site (latest site check), seen in the latest Pages file, that file's impressions with its date range, Optimize suggestions accepted (from search_optimizations) and a planned line for AI answers and communities. Search Everywhere's page payload adds contentUpdates (gscContentUpdates): every Distributed release plus gap and window priorities marked done. Rules held: PR approval never waits on a search score; Search Everywhere never edits or publishes a release; the five-priority limit is unchanged.

Impressions are not "since publishing"

Search Console exports have no per-page daily data and the Pages export carries no period in the files loaded so far. The strip and Content updates show the page's impressions in the latest Pages file with the dates when known, and say when the file may include days before publishing.
