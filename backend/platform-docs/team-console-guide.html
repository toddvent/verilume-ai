For Verilume staff. Matches the 2026-11-25 release (r114).

For Verilume staff. How to sign in to the Team Console, find a client, change their plan, record agreements and invoices, and manage the team. It covers what each person can and cannot do and what to do when something looks wrong.

Signing in

  1. Open the Team Console at /platform. Enter your work email and password.
  2. Choose how to receive your 6-digit code: email, or text if you have turned text sign-in on.
  3. Enter the code. Codes last 10 minutes. Your session lasts 12 hours, then you sign in again.

Turning on text sign-in

Click My mobile in the top bar, enter your number, enter the code we text you, and it is on. Only you can turn it on, because you have to prove the number is yours. An Owner or Admin can turn it off for someone (that also clears the number). Password reset is still email only.

First time

New team members get a setup link. Choose a password and confirm your email code. Your Owner sets your role.

Working in a client's workspace

Go to Accounts & workspaces, find the client and click Open. Everything you change is written to the Access log with your name.

Plan & Billing

  • Shows the client's current level and footprint. Change the level as described below.
  • Invoices: issue an invoice (numbered INV-1001 and up per client) and mark it Sent, Paid or Overdue. Marking Paid stamps today's date. No payment is taken or moved here; this is a record only.

Data & Connections

Set the analytics connection to Not connected, Pending client grant or Connected, and enter the GA4 account and property ids. This is the same record the client sees in their portal.

Team Access

Lists the client's own team members. Use Deactivate when a client asks you to remove someone or for troubleshooting, and Activate to restore. The person is not deleted.

Agreements

Four are tracked for every client: Mutual NDA, Contract / SOW, Terms of Service, IP Ownership & Assignment Addendum. Set each to Draft, Sent or Signed; dates are recorded. The signed file itself is not stored here, so keep it in your document system. There is no e-signature in the console.

Changing a client's level

  1. Open the client and go to Plan & Billing.
  2. Pick the new level. Any level above the free trial needs the IO reference (the number on the signed insertion order).
  3. Tick Terms accepted through the signed IO if the IO incorporates the Terms. This records the acceptance.
  4. Save. The level badge updates and the change is logged. The client's portal reflects it on next load.

Premium commitment tiers (Flex, Quarterly, Annual) are not recorded in the console yet.

Managing the team

  1. Open Team (Owner or Admin).
  2. Enter name, email and role in Add a team member and click Add. They receive a setup link.
  3. To remove access, set the person to inactive in the Verilume team table.

What each role can do

RoleCan do
OwnerEverything, including archive, restore and delete client accounts, demo reset and team management
AdminTeam management and every staff panel; cannot delete client accounts
ITArchive, restore and delete client accounts
SupportStaff panels and client changes
Read-onlyView only; every change is refused

Since the 2026-11-25 release, all roles except Read-only can use every staff panel. If you need a role limited further, tell the Owner.

House rules

  • Never share your sign-in or code. Do not forward a code.
  • Open a client only when you have a reason; access is logged and reviewed.
  • Do not copy client data out of the console into email or chat. Share the portal link instead.
  • Use view as only to reproduce a problem a client reported. It lasts 4 hours and is logged.
  • Never type a client's password. We never need it.
  • Report a lost phone or laptop to the Owner immediately so your session and number can be cleared.
